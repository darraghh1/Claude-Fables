Found in WO-002 (Terminal band: draw Fables as a Raster) live smoke. renderer/frames.ts with look 'pixel' (or no look, which is the default pixel) renders every frame of SAMPLES[0] at 220x15 as a flat dark stage: per-frame mean channel 46.7, max 56, identical from frame 0 to 42. Every other look renders the scene (original mean 88.7 max 251, ukiyoe 140.6/255, aero 138.2/255, ...). Repro: bun a script that pipes {scene: SAMPLES[0], columns: 220, rows: 15, look: 'pixel', fps: 12, seconds: 1} into renderer/frames.ts and averages the decoded cell colours. Suspect: the sc-pixelize filter (hooks/svg.ts PIXELIZE: feFlood/feTile grid + feComposite + feMorphology) in Chromium when the SVG is decoded as an <img> and drawn to an OffscreenCanvas at RASTER 0.5. renderer/smoke.ts did not catch it: it passes no look and only asks for >=10 distinct frames, which near-black frames with tiny variation satisfy; a brightness/contrast check there would. WO-002 works around it in hooks/terminal/player.ts:terminalLook (pixel draws as original in the terminal).
