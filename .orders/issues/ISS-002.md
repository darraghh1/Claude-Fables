Found at end of session 2026-10-03: a headless Chromium (pid 350606, user-data-dir /tmp/fables-frames-350578-YVSw5J) had been running orphaned for ~11 h, reparented to systemd --user, plus 3 stale /tmp/fables-frames-* dirs. The helper launches Chromium detached in its own process group and kills the group on exit/SIGTERM/SIGINT/EPIPE, but a SIGKILLed helper (engine kill, OOM, crash) runs no cleanup, so the group survives. Killed by the lead. Fix direction: tie the browser's life to the helper (e.g. PR_SET_PDEATHSIG-style parent watch in a tiny wrapper, or Chromium polling its parent pid / stdin pipe), and have the helper sweep stale fables-frames-<pid> dirs whose pid is gone. Repro: start bun renderer/frames.ts on a long job, kill -9 its pid, pgrep -f fables-frames.
