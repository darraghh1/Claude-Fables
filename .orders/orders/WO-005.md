## Intent

Darragh asked to try quadrant glyphs after WO-003 made pixel art pixel-exact. Pixel art is already at its native resolution, but the painted looks have detail finer than one terminal pixel. A 2×2 quadrant cell doubles horizontal resolution at the cost of two colours per cell. Done means the painted looks reconstruct their full-resolution image measurably better, pixel art is unchanged, and Darragh can switch it off.

## Specification

Repo state (main): renderer/cells.ts:packHalfBlocks packs columns × rows*2 RGB into ▀ cells (fg top, bg bottom). renderer/frames.ts renders at 1 device px per stage unit and downsamples per look, point-sampled for pixel and area-filtered for painted looks. The mod treats cells as opaque until it blends them: hooks/terminal/compose.ts:blend mixes fg/bg per cell for the entrance cross-fade, and hooks/terminal/loop.ts:wrapBlend for the loop wrap. Both assume every cell is ▀.

Change:
1. renderer/cells.ts: add `packQuadrants(rgb, columns, rows)` over a columns*2 × rows*2 image. For each 2×2 block, choose the split of its 4 subpixels into two groups that minimises squared error against the two group means, out of the 8 distinct partitions. Emit the matching BMP glyph from U+2580–U+259F (▀ ▄ ▌ ▐ ▖ ▗ ▘ ▝ ▚ ▞ ▙ ▛ ▜ ▟ and █, or ' ' for a flat block) with fg = the 'ink' group mean and bg = the other. Keep it pure.
2. renderer/frames.ts: the job gains `glyphs: 'half' | 'quadrant'` (default 'half'). For 'quadrant', sample to columns*2 × rows*2 and pack with packQuadrants. Pixel look is always 'half'.
3. Mod: userConfig `terminalGlyphs: 'auto' | 'half' | 'quadrant'` (default 'auto' = quadrant for every look but pixel). hooks/terminal/player.ts passes it in the job. compose.ts:blend and loop.ts wrap-blend must stay valid across glyph changes: where two cells' glyphs differ, take the from-cell below weight 0.5 and the to-cell at or above it. Where glyphs match, blend fg and bg as today.
4. smoke.ts: a reconstruction check (below) and the existing budget check run at 'quadrant' for a painted look too.

Measure: reconstruction MAE is the mean absolute channel error between what the cells display (each cell expanded to its 2×2 subpixels by its glyph, fg and bg) and the frame sampled at columns*2 × rows*2 with the same filter. Half-block cells expand each subpixel row from one colour per cell.

Derived requirements: M-01, M-04, M-06 as before; M-05 is unchanged; M-02/M-03: desktop and model calls untouched.

## Non-goals

- Pixel look in quadrants: its art is already one terminal pixel per art pixel, so quadrants add nothing.
- Braille or sextant glyphs: braille is one colour per cell, and sextants are outside the BMP, so the Raster refuses them.
- Working around the engine's colour-pair rounding.
